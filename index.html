How an engagement runs

Forward deployed means we work where the problem is. Each stage ends with something your team can use.

  1. Embed

    Spend the first weeks with the people who make the decision. Map how it's made today and what a better answer is worth.

  2. Connect

    Wire up the source systems and build a data model named in your team's own terms, not the database's.

  3. Ship a pilot

    Put a working tool in front of real users on a live project, then improve it weekly from what they tell us.

  4. Harden and hand over

    Add security, monitoring, and documentation, and train your team to run and extend it without us.

What we hold to
